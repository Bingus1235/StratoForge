About StratoForge

StratoForge is a student-led rocketry team dedicated to competing in the American Rocketry Challenge (ARC).

Our team brings together students from diverse backgrounds, united under a shared purpose.

Through this journey, we learn hands on experience in aerospace engineering, teamwork, and problem-solving, while inspiring the next generation of innovators.

Our Mission

To design, build, and successfully launch competitive rockets while fostering unity in our community.

Our Vision

To become a nationally recognized team in rocketry competitions.
